About Vuilgat

Our History

Vuilgat started as a thought and became a reality in 2014, born purely out of a deep-rooted love for cars and the desire to create an identity that enthusiasts could proudly wear and display. Specialising in statement t-shirts and custom car stickers tailored for the local stance car community, the brand evolved from a passion project into a recognised name spotted across various locations on numerous cars.

Building on its growing street credibility, Vuilgat has expanded further into the car culture scene, now officially featured on promotional posters when hosting and participating in major car events.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To unite and authentically represent car enthusiasts and the local stance culture through high-quality apparel and custom vehicle decals.

Vision

To establish Vuilgat as a widely recognised, prominent lifestyle brand with a strong digital footprint and e-commerce presence — growing t-shirt merchandise alongside our popular car stickers to build a major brand.

Who We Make This For

  • Car enthusiasts and local motorists passionate about automotive lifestyle culture.
  • Owners and drivers of stance cars who customise their vehicles.
  • Urban youth and subculture participants who connect with local streetwear and identity expression.

What We Stand For

Authenticity

Every design comes out of the local stance scene, not off a trend board.

Quality

Small-batch printing on heavyweight cotton, decals built to survive daily driving.

Community

We show up at events, sponsor builds, and print what the scene actually wants to wear.
